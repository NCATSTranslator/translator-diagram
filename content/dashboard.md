# Translator components

The published dashboard's table with nothing withheld, as one file. Generated by `build-content` from the same payload the page is built from; each component links to its own page under [`components/`](components/), and [`deployments.csv`](deployments.csv) has every field of every cell.

Of 49 deployments, 30 have a version from OpenAPI, 8 from SmartAPI, 3 from Helm, and 8 from nothing at all. 4 are absent from their component's SmartAPI record, which does list other environments.

7 disagree across environments: [shepherd-aragorn](components/shepherd-aragorn.md), [shepherd-arax](components/shepherd-arax.md), [arax](components/arax.md), [shepherd-bte](components/shepherd-bte.md), [answer-appraiser](components/answer-appraiser.md), [node-annotator](components/node-annotator.md), [name-lookup](components/name-lookup.md).

A cell reads `version (source)`. `?` is deployed with no version found; `—` is not deployed there; `\*` marks a version in the minority for its row.
